Privacy Policy

FieldReport does not collect, receive, or share any personal data.

Everything you create in the app — clients, work reports, photos, invoices and your company details — is stored locally on your device. The app has no server component and no network code: it cannot transmit your data anywhere, and the developer never has access to it.

Data Collection

  • No user accounts
  • No analytics
  • No tracking, and no data used for advertising
  • No third-party data sharing

Client Details You Enter

Reports can include a client’s name, address, phone number and email address. You enter this information yourself, it stays on your device, and it is used only to print the invoices you generate. If you record details about other people, you are responsible for having their permission to do so.

Camera and Photos

FieldReport uses the device camera and photo library only to allow users to create work reports with photos. Photos are saved locally on the device and remain under the user’s control.

Purchases

All subscriptions and payments are processed securely by Apple via the App Store. FieldReport does not collect or have access to payment information.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data never leaves the device, you control how long it is kept, and there is nothing for the developer to retain or delete on your behalf:

  • Delete an individual report or client at any time from inside the app.
  • Deleting FieldReport from your device permanently removes every report, photo, invoice and company detail it stored.
  • Bank account, routing number and tax ID are held in the iOS Keychain and are removed together with the app.
  • Invoices you have already exported or shared (by mail, Messages or AirDrop) leave the app and are no longer governed by this policy.
  • If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, your FieldReport data is included in that backup and is governed by Apple’s terms.

You can withdraw camera or photo library access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security, or in Settings → FieldReport. The app keeps working without those permissions; you simply cannot attach new photos.

Children

FieldReport is a business tool for contractors and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly process any data from children.

Changes
